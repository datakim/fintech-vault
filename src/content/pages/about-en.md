I work as a data scientist in the fintech organisation of an e-commerce company, and teach financial AI as an adjunct professor at Ajou University's graduate school of AI.

This blog collects notes on financial AI, credit scoring, LLM applications, and the problems that turn up once any of it reaches production.

Most posts are written in Korean. The [Posts(ENG)](/en/) section carries English versions of selected pieces.
